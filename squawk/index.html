ATC

Squawk AI air traffic control for MSFS 2024 on Xbox

1. Load your SimBrief flight

Plan the flight on simbrief.com and import that same plan into MSFS 2024 with the SimBrief button on the World Map. Then the sim and this app fly the same route.

This page can't reach SimBrief directly. Do this instead: open your latest SimBrief plan, select all the text, copy it, come back, and paste it below.

Or paste a route

Copy the route from SimBrief (or any flight plan) and paste it here. It should start with your departure airport and runway and end with your arrival, like KSAN/27 N0497F330 ZZOOO4 MTBAL ... NUGR1H EGLL/09L.

Or enter the flight by hand

Settings

Azure voices (optional, most human)

Paste your Azure Speech key and region. They stay on this device only and never go into the code on GitHub. Free tier: 500,000 characters a month, roughly 100 flights.

Voice tip: Claude's natural voices work when this page is open in Chrome or the Claude desktop app. Accents come from voices installed on your device, and the free ones often sound robotic. On iPhone, download better ones in Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices. Pick voices marked Premium or Enhanced (English UK, Australia, India, Ireland, plus French, German, Spanish). Squawk uses those first. On Android, install "Speech Recognition and Synthesis from Google" and download languages in its settings.

How this works with the Xbox

Xbox doesn't let outside apps read the sim, so Squawk can't see your plane. You keep it in sync two ways: you talk to it like real ATC, and you tap the gold event buttons (like "Airborne" or "Top of descent") when those things happen in the sim. Turn off the sim's own ATC voice so you don't hear two controllers. Tune the frequencies Squawk gives you on COM1 so your cockpit matches.
