About the program

Opening the hangar doors for students who never knew they could walk in.

Aero Xperience is a program run by Black Women in Aviation, a nonprofit. It brings aviation straight to students in North Carolina and Delaware, so they can see the jobs up close instead of just reading about them.

The idea is simple. A lot of kids think aviation means one job: pilot. In reality there are mechanics, engineers, dispatchers, drone operators, and many more roles on the ground and in the air. Aero Xperience puts students in the same room as those people and lets them try the work for themselves.

The program's motto sums it up: Expanding Access. Inspiring Futures. Building the Next Generation of Aviators.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Who runs Aero Xperience?

Black Women in Aviation, a nonprofit that works to open aviation careers to more people. Its founder, Samantha Mitchell, was the first female maintenance technician at JFK International Airport.

Do I need to know anything about aviation to go?

No. The whole point is to give students a first look. Every station is hands-on and built for beginners.

Where is the 2026 event?

At the Expo Center in Durham, NC, on Saturday, October 31, 2026, from 8 AM to 8 PM. Check the official page for registration details.

Is it only about becoming a pilot?

Not at all. Students meet mechanics, engineers, dispatchers, drone operators, and other workers. Many great aviation jobs never leave the ground.

How do I find out about the Delaware event?

Join the mailing list on the official Aero Xperience page. Delaware is planned for March 2027, and details will be shared there first.
